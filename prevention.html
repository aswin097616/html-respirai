Respiratory Disease Prevention

Protecting your respiratory system is crucial for long-term health. Follow these expert-recommended guidelines to minimize your exposure to pathogens and irritants that can lead to acute or chronic lung conditions.

  • 🚭
    Immediate Smoking Cessation: This is the single most important action to prevent COPD, emphysema, and lung cancer. Avoid all forms of tobacco and electronic cigarettes.
  • 💉
    Maintain Up-to-Date Vaccinations: Ensure you receive the annual flu shot, as well as pneumococcal and COVID-19 vaccines, as recommended by public health guidelines.
  • 🧼
    Strict Hand Hygiene: Wash hands frequently for at least 20 seconds, especially before eating and after being in public places, to limit the transmission of respiratory viruses.
  • 🏡
    Control Indoor Air Quality: Use HEPA filters to reduce allergens and pollutants. Ensure proper ventilation, especially when using cleaning chemicals or cooking.
  • 💪
    Regular Physical Activity: Exercise strengthens the respiratory muscles and improves lung capacity, making the body more resilient to illness.
